# Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS)

## Introduction

Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS) is a type of cloud computing. It allows people and companies to rent computing resources such as servers, storage, and networks through the internet. They do not need to buy expensive physical servers to use these resources.

## How IaaS Works

A cloud provider owns and maintains the physical hardware. The customer chooses the resources they need and uses them to run software or store data. The customer usually manages the operating system, applications, and data. Many IaaS services charge customers based on their usage.

## Example

A small business can rent a virtual server from Amazon EC2 to host its website. If more people visit the website, the business can increase its computing resources instead of buying a new server.

## Advantages

* Reduces the need to buy and maintain physical hardware.
* Allows resources to increase or decrease when needed.
* Makes it easier to start and test new applications.

## Conclusion

IaaS is useful because it gives businesses flexible access to computing resources. It can save time and reduce initial costs, but customers must still manage their software and protect their data.
